# v1.5.2 — Visualizer redesign, settings About block, theme dropdown

## Visualizer (grid)
- 12 → 24 columns; 6-stop palette ramp weighted to the low end (softer bloom, no banding).
- Per-launch randomized idle start (`idleOffset`), 4 new idle fields (ember heart, radial pulse, falling motes, fbm bloom) — 12 fields on a 12-slot schedule, no self-dissolve including wrap.
- `_tile` overdraw trimmed (halo only on bright tiles, lip/foot skipped on dim) to hold the 16 ms budget at 2× tile count. `node tools/test-viz.js` → 30/30.
